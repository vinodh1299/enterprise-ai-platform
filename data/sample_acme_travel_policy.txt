================================================================================
               ACME ENTERPRISE GLOBAL TRAVEL & IT EQUIPMENT POLICY (2025)
================================================================================

SECTION 1: BUSINESS TRAVEL & FLIGHT REIMBURSEMENTS
1.1 Air Travel Class:
    - All domestic and short-haul flights under 6 hours must be booked in Economy Class.
    - International flights exceeding 6 hours in duration are eligible for Business Class booking with prior manager approval.

1.2 Lodging & Hotel Allowances:
    - Standard domestic hotel budget is capped at $250.00 USD per night (excluding taxes).
    - Tier-1 global cities (New York, London, Tokyo, San Francisco) are permitted up to $350.00 USD per night.

1.3 Meal & Incidentals Allowance (Per Diem):
    - Domestic Meal Allowance: $75.00 USD per day ($15 Breakfast, $25 Lunch, $35 Dinner).
    - International Meal Allowance: $120.00 USD per day ($25 Breakfast, $40 Lunch, $55 Dinner).
    - Alcohol is strictly non-reimbursable under company policy.

SECTION 2: IT EQUIPMENT & REMOTE WORK STIPEND
2.1 Hardware Provisioning:
    - All Senior Software and AI Engineers are issued a 16-inch Apple MacBook Pro (M3 Max, 36GB RAM, 1TB SSD).
    - Monitors and docking stations are provided at corporate offices.

2.2 Home Office & Ergonomics Allowance:
    - Full-time remote employees receive a one-time $500.00 USD home office setup stipend.
    - High-speed home internet is reimbursed up to $80.00 USD per month with receipt proof.

SECTION 3: EXPENSE SUBMISSION & APPROVAL DEADLINES
3.1 Expense Reporting:
    - All expense reports must be submitted within 14 business days of returning from trip.
    - Expense items exceeding $25.00 USD require itemized physical or digital receipts.
    - Manager approval is required for all expense reports prior to Finance payout.
